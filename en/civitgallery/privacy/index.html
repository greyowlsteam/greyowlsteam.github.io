CivitGallery privacy

No ads, analytics or trackers. You don't need an account to browse.

Only on your phone

Theme, tile size and feed filters, the image and depth map cache, sign-in tokens (encrypted with a key in Android Keystore). App data is excluded from Android backups.

Civitai

Images and their data are loaded directly from civitai.com. If you sign in with Civitai, you do it on Civitai's own page — your password never passes through the app. After that your reactions, collections, follows and hidden tags are read and changed in your Civitai account, under Civitai's privacy policy.

Our server (extended tags)

After you accept the rules, the app creates a guest profile with:

  • a device key — only the public part; the private key is created in Android Keystore and never leaves the phone;
  • the phone model name and the interface language;
  • your age confirmation and content consents, with the rules version and date;
  • a random nickname (user-123456) that you can change;
  • the tags you add, your votes and reports, and the IDs of the Civitai images they belong to;
  • when you hide extended tags, the IDs of those tags and of the images on the feed page, to find which images to hide or blur;
  • your app settings: hidden tags and authors, extended tags to hide, ratings, consents and video sound — so they come back after reinstalling;
  • only if you link them: your email (the sign-in code is sent by email; only a hash of the code is kept) and your Telegram ID, username and name.

Like any web server, it keeps technical request logs (time, request address, IP address) to run the service and protect it from abuse.

GitHub

Once a day the app asks GitHub for the latest release, and depth models are downloaded from GitHub when you tap the button. No personal data is sent; GitHub sees your IP address.

Deleting your data

Settings → App profile → Delete profile deletes your votes, reports, sign-in methods, device keys and saved settings. Tags you added stay on the images without your name. To remove data from the phone, uninstall the app. To disconnect Civitai, sign out in Settings → Civitai account.

This site

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages: no cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub, as the host, sees visitors' IP addresses.
